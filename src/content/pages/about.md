---
heading: About Fernway
subheading: One roaster, one café, and a stubborn belief that fresh beats fancy.
---

Fernway began in 2021 as a market stall with a borrowed grinder. These days we
roast every batch ourselves and serve it in a small room with big windows.

We keep the list short on purpose: a handful of coffees we actually love,
changed often, explained plainly. If you're new to any of it, ask — that's the
best part of the job.
