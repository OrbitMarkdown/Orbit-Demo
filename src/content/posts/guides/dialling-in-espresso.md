Every morning starts the same way: a few shots that nobody drinks, while we get
the grinder where it needs to be. It sounds fussy. It's really just three numbers.

![From fine espresso grind to coarse press grind](../../../assets/posts/content-grind.svg)

## The three numbers

- **Dose** — how much ground coffee goes in (we use 18g).
- **Yield** — how much liquid comes out (about 36g).
- **Time** — how long that takes (25–30 seconds).

Change **one** of them at a time. Too fast and sour? Grind finer. Too slow and
bitter? Grind coarser. That's most of it.
