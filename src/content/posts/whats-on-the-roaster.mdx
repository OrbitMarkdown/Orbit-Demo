## The bright one — Ethiopia Guji

Our lightest roast: peach, jasmine, black tea. Best as filter. There's a whole
post on it if you want the detail.

<Callout kind="note" title="Roasted fresh on Sundays">
We roast this one every Sunday, so a bag bought early in the week is about as
fresh as coffee gets.
</Callout>

## The balanced one — Colombia Huila

Milk chocolate, red apple, a clean finish. Happy as filter or espresso — the
crowd-pleaser of the three.

![A fresh bag, roasted this week](../../assets/posts/content-bag.svg)

## The milk one — House Espresso

Our darker blend: cocoa and toasted nut, built to punch through steamed milk.
It's what's in your flat white.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Not sure which to pick?">
Tell us how you brew and how you take it, and we'll point you straight at the
right bag. That's the best part of the counter.
</Callout>
