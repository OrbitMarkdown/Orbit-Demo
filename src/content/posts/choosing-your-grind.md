If one thing separates a flat cup from a bright one, it's grind size. Same beans,
same water — grind them differently and you get a different drink.

![From fine espresso grind to coarse press grind](../../assets/posts/content-grind.svg)

The rule of thumb: **the faster the water passes through, the finer you grind.**

- **Espresso** — very fine, like caster sugar. Water's only in contact for
  seconds, so it needs the extra surface.
- **Filter / pour-over** — medium, like table salt. Our house method.
- **French press** — coarse, like breadcrumbs. It steeps for minutes, so a fine
  grind would turn it muddy and bitter.

Dial it by taste, not numbers: sour and thin means grind finer; harsh and bitter
means grind coarser. Change one thing at a time and you'll find it fast.
